Static sign-up banner

QA20261110-4 · epic QA-20261110-S5. This prototype is the whole journey of the story: the product manager opens one static, desktop-only sign-up banner — a headline, one email field and a "Sign up" button — looks it over, and approves it or asks for a change. Nothing is built, sent or stored: clicking "Sign up" keeps you on the same screen.

Screens

  • Sign-up banner

    New screen. Exactly three elements: headline, one email field with sample text, "Sign up" button. Default state only.

Choices made in this prototype

  • Headline copy: "Get product updates in your inbox" — the working choice from the approved structure; change it at review if you have other wording.
  • Look: colours, type and spacing copied from play1's approved sign-up design (story 215fd057). System fonts only, no third-party fonts, icons or images, so no license note is needed.
  • Placement: the banner stands alone, centred on a plain desktop page, with no site header or footer around it.
  • "Sign up" is a plain button outside any form: clicking it does nothing and stays on this screen. The email field shows only the sample placeholder name@example.com; anything typed is not stored.
  • The email field has no visible label, to keep exactly three elements; it carries an accessible name for screen readers instead.
  • The thin link bar above the banner is prototype navigation only, not part of the design.
