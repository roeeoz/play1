Choices made in this prototype
  • Direction — Cards: the choice is its own labelled step under the epic box: two option cards with an icon each and a radio mark, and the line under them. It is more visible and easier to hit, at the cost of a taller page; the box itself is untouched.
  • Click the two options on New Epic: the selection and the line under it switch (radio buttons and CSS, no script). “PRD” is selected when the page opens.
  • Line wording: “The agent drafts the PRD.” / “The agent drafts the PRD and the stories.”, as assumed in the approved structure.
  • Everything else is today's page (NewEpicPage.tsx): box, Cancel, Create epic, Jira and Confluence pills, help text. Create epic keeps today's blue: the story asks that nothing else changes, so the teal brand rule is not applied here.
  • Cancel and Create epic lead to Workspace and the epic's conversation, which have no page; they are drawn, not linked. The Halo logo in the sidebar leads back here.
  • Next step draws New Epic in its two states, “PRD” selected and “PRD and stories” selected, for desktop and phone.
  • Thumbnail: desktop browser at 1280×800; the story names no device.
