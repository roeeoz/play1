Newsletter sign-up — Prototype

This prototype covers the happy-path journey: a visitor arrives at the Welcome screen, continues to the sign-up form, enters their email address and consent, and reaches the Confirmation screen. Click through the screens below or start the journey from the beginning.

  • Welcome Explains the newsletter and invites the visitor to sign up.
  • Sign-up form Collects the email address and consent before subscribing.
  • Confirmation Tells the visitor to check their inbox to confirm the subscription.
