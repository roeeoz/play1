Choices made in this prototype

Direction

Split layout: every screen pairs a teal context panel (a sample issue, what you are signing up for, what happens next) with the task beside it, and a numbered step bar in the header. On phone the panel stacks above the task.

Interactions chosen

  • “Subscribe” goes straight to Confirmation: the prototype has no script, so it shows the path where the email is valid, consent is ticked and the submission succeeds.
  • The form opens with a sample address filled in and the consent box unticked; tick it to walk the path as a visitor would.
  • “Back” on the form returns to Welcome; “Back to the start” on Confirmation is the way back.
  • Primary buttons are teal #0F766E with white text, full-width on phone (standing brand rule).

What the next step draws

  • Sign-up form in every state: invalid email (error beside the field), consent missing, loading (button disabled, spinner), submission failed (input kept, banner with “Try again”).
  • Every screen at desktop 1280×800 and phone 390×844.

Assumptions

  • No product screen exists to copy, so the look is new: system font stack (no product font in any checkout), one palette and type scale.
  • Sample content names the product Halo and the newsletter “Halo Release Notes”.
  • Consent text is a placeholder to be confirmed before release.
  • Confirmation says a welcome email was sent; it does not promise a confirm-your-address step, which the story does not ask for.
