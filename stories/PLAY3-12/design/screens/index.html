Choices and assumptions

  • Drawn from concept A: one compact row under the box and above the Jira and Confluence pills, with the label, a pill-shaped two-way switch and the line in small grey text.
  • The switch is one radio group, and both options are live on every page. The two states differ only in which option is selected when the page opens.
  • Halo has no phone layout today. The mobile pages show the sidebar as Halo’s own collapsed icon rail and the same page column at 390 px, with tighter outer padding, a 28 px title and the pills wrapping.
  • “Create epic” keeps today’s blue (#0073AA) and its place in the box’s action row on mobile too. The product memory’s teal, full-width primary button rule is not applied, because the story says everything else on the page stays as it is today. This was already approved in the prototype.
  • The states the page already has (Creating…, the creation error, the empty-name error) are not drawn because the inventory doesn’t name them. While the epic is being created, the switch is disabled like the other fields.
  • “Cancel” leads back to this gallery. “Create epic” is drawn but not linked, because the epic conversation stays as it is today and has no page.
