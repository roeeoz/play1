Choices made in this prototype

Concept A · Compact

  • One quiet row in the pills’ type and padding: the label “Create drafts for”, a pill-shaped two-way switch, and the line in small grey text under it.
  • Adds the least height to the page, so it reads as one more setting of the epic, like the board and the spaces.

Shared by A and B

  • The choice sits under the box (name, description, Cancel, Create epic) and above the Jira and Confluence pills, as the structure places it.
  • “PRD” is selected at first. The line under the choice reads “The agent drafts the PRD.” or “The agent drafts the PRD and the stories.”
  • It is one radio group: clicking an option selects it and swaps the line. No other field, label or button changes.
  • “Create epic” keeps today’s blue (#0073AA). The teal brand rule in product memory is not applied, because the story says everything else stays as it is today.
  • Workspace and Epic conversation stay as they are today and get no page. “Cancel” leads back here, and “Create epic” is drawn but not linked.
  • Next step: the New Epic page in both states (“PRD” selected and “PRD and stories” selected) for desktop and phone.
