Concept B · Cards

The outline is a set of three cards under the New Epic composer, each naming the headings it brings; the confirmation opens as a callout pointing at the card the PO picked. The PO reaches New Epic from the Workspace’s sidebar, as it is today, and leaves it by Create epic or Cancel, also as today.

New Epic

Outline cards added under the composer; Blank on, box empty

Picks “New capability”
STEP 02
New Epic (outline selected)

New capability on; its headings fill the box

Writes own text, picks “Change to an existing screen”
STEP 03
New Epic (confirm replace)

Own text in the box; replace callout under the cards

Picks “Blank”: the page’s headings are cleared

Each screen is shown at desktop 1280×720, landscape — the viewport the approved structure names (Playwright’s Desktop Chrome, the only device the product’s tests use); the New Epic page has no phone layout, so none is drawn. Steps 02 and 03 are states of the one New Epic screen: the confirmation and the filled outline show only there.

Choices made in this prototype
  • Where the outline sits: a “Start from an outline” group of three cards directly under the rounded composer, above the Jira and Confluence pills; each card lists the headings it puts in, so the PO picks knowing what they get.
  • Confirmation: an amber callout under the cards, its pointer at the picked card, naming the headings that will replace the text, with Keep my text and Replace text; the picked card shows dashed until they answer.
  • One choice, Blank first: the three outlines are a single radio group; the page opens on Blank with the description empty (AC 1).
  • Filling: on a box that is empty or holds only the page’s headings, an outline puts its headings in at once (AC 2); Blank clears the page’s headings. Headings are Markdown ## lines: New capability — Problem, Users, What they can do, Out of scope; Change to an existing screen — Screen, What changes, Why, Out of scope.
  • Own text: any text the PO typed, edited headings included, makes any other outline — Blank too — ask before replacing it (AC 3). Keep my text leaves the text and the previous outline; Replace text puts the new headings in.
  • Buttons: Replace text is outlined in the error red because it clears text; Keep my text is the neutral, safe one. Neither is a primary action, so the brand’s teal primary rule (#0F766E) is not used here; Create epic keeps today’s blue — the story does not change it.
  • Box height: once an outline fills it the description grows to 230 px so every heading shows (today it is 110 px and would scroll).
  • What is clickable: the outline options, the confirmation’s buttons (in-page anchors show the kept or replaced result) and the sidebar’s New Epic, which reopens a fresh page. Create epic (to the Epic conversation), Cancel (to the Workspace) and the rest of the sidebar lead to screens that stay as they are today and are drawn, not linked.
  • Font: the product’s Assistant Variable is not in the checkout, so the pages use the stack’s system-ui fallback.
  • Walking to step 03: a prototype cannot read typing, so on step 02 a click into the description fills it with sample text of the PO’s own; “Change to an existing screen” then opens the confirmation.
  • Next step draws: New Epic in its three states — default, outline selected, confirm replace — at desktop 1280×720.
