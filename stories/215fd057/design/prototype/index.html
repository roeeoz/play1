Newsletter sign-up

This prototype walks through the newsletter sign-up journey: a visitor arrives at the Welcome screen, continues to the sign-up form where they enter their email address and give consent, then sees a Confirmation screen. Click through the screens below to follow the happy path.

Screens

  • Welcome
    Explains the newsletter and invites the visitor to sign up.
  • Sign-up form
    Collects email address and explicit consent.
  • Confirmation
    Tells the visitor to check their inbox.
