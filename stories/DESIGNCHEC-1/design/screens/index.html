Newsletter sign-up — screens

Every screen of the approved structure in every state, desktop and mobile side by side. The look follows the approved prototype and today's Sign-up form on The Harbour Gazette (harbour-signup-today.png): burgundy header, cream page, serif type, bordered white card, yellow uppercase button. All pages share tokens.css. Inside the pages, the links follow the journey: Sign up → form; Subscribe → loading; Back → Welcome.

Design choices

  • Today's form has no error style, so this story adds one error red (--color-error): the field or box gets a red outline and a message with an icon directly below the control. That is never colour alone.
  • In the loading state the button reads "Subscribing…" with a spinner and can't be pressed. The field and box stay visible but are locked, and Back is paused until the answer comes.
  • Submission failed: the message sits at the top of the card, the input is kept, and Subscribe is the retry (no extra button), as the structure decided.
  • On mobile the "Past dispatches" column stacks under the card, and the buttons fill the width at 48px tall. Every control is at least 44px.
  • "Back to the site" leads to Welcome, standing in for the public site home page, as in the prototype. Stories and Archive in the header are shown as they are today but lead nowhere.
