Newsletter sign-up — prototype

A clickable walk through the main path of the approved structure: a visitor to The Harbour Gazette reads what the newsletter is, signs up with an email address and an explicit consent, and is told to check their inbox. The Sign-up form is rebuilt from today's page (harbour-signup-today.png) and keeps its look; Welcome and Confirmation are new and use the same header, palette, type and footer. Every page also works at phone width.

Prototype choices

  • Subscribe always goes to Confirmation here; the validation errors, the loading state and the submission-failed state are drawn in the next step.
  • The checkbox can be ticked but nothing checks it; it starts unticked, as it must.
  • "Back to the site" leads to Welcome, standing in for the public site home page, which is outside this story.
  • In the site header only "Subscribe" is a link (to Welcome); Stories and Archive are shown as they are today but lead nowhere in the prototype.
