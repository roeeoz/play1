Choices made in this screen set
  • States follow the structure's inventory and diagram: the inline error state is drawn three ways (name, email, both) as the diagram shows; Confirmation has only a default state.
  • Button reads “Join the newsletter” as approved in the prototype; its loading label is “Joining…” (the inventory's “Signing up…” predates the rename).
  • Monthly digest checkbox is unticked by default; the error, loading and submit-error states show it ticked to prove the choice is kept. Confirmation shows the ticked variant; when unticked, the line “You'll also get the monthly digest.” is simply left out — no separate page.
  • Focus on the first invalid field is drawn as a red focus ring (the page also sets autofocus there).
  • Links: “Join the newsletter” leads to the loading state; “Back to the guestbook” and the logo open this gallery because there is no guestbook page yet (the real target is a placeholder /).
  • Phone layout: the card drops its border and shadow and fills the width; fields and button get 14 px vertical padding for touch.
