Newsletter sign-up — clickable prototype

This prototype walks the main path of the approved structure: a visitor to the public site reads what the newsletter is on the Welcome screen, enters an email address and ticks the consent on the Sign-up form, and lands on the Confirmation screen that tells them to check their inbox. No account, no sign-in. Every page works on desktop and on a phone-width screen.

Start the journey

Screens

  1. Welcome — default state, with the latest release mentioned
  2. Sign-up form — sample email address filled in; the visitor ticks the consent
  3. Confirmation — default state, names the address

Choices made for the prototype

  • "Subscribe" goes straight to Confirmation. The loading state, the validation errors (invalid email, consent missing, both) and the failed submission are drawn in the next step.
  • The email field and the consent checkbox can be typed in and ticked, but the Confirmation page always names the sample address dana.levi@example.com.
  • The consent checkbox starts unticked, with its full wording beside it.
  • "Back to the site" on Confirmation leads to the public home page, which is outside this prototype; here it returns to this index.
