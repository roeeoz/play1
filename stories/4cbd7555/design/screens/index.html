Choices made in this set

  • The layout, header, progress bar and palette follow the approved prototype; shared styles live in tokens.css. Desktop and phone pages are separate files; phone pages use full-width buttons, tighter spacing and a smaller heading.
  • Every control is at least 44 px tall; the consent checkbox is 24 px with its whole label tappable. Nothing is wider than the phone viewport.
  • Validation errors are shown below each control (the field's message under the field, the consent message under the checkbox with a red bar beside the group), in words with an icon, never by colour alone.
  • In Loading the email field is read-only and the checkbox locked while the button shows a spinner and "Subscribing…"; nothing can be sent twice.
  • In Submission failed the message names the cause in plain words; there is no separate "Try again" button — Subscribe is enabled again, as agreed in the structure.
  • Confirmation always names the sample address dana.levi@example.com and looks the same whether or not the address was already on the list.
  • "Subscribe" links to the Loading state; the review bar on Loading offers the two answers (success → Confirmation, failure → Submission failed), and on the default form the three validation outcomes. "Return to homepage" on Confirmation (and "Back to the site" on Welcome) lead to the public home page, outside this set; here they return to this gallery.
