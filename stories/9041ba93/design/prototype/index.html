Newsletter sign-up prototype

This prototype covers the main path of the newsletter sign-up journey: a visitor arrives at the Welcome screen, continues to the sign-up form, enters their email address with consent, and reaches the Confirmation screen. Error and empty states are covered in the full design step.

Screens

  • Welcome
    Explains the newsletter and invites the visitor to sign up.
  • Sign-up form
    Collects an email address and explicit consent.
  • Confirmation
    Tells the visitor to check their inbox.
